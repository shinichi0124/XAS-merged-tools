## XAFSmass — Henke f″ tables

- Used for: the `HENKE`, `EDGE_DATA` and `XT_MU` tables in the HTML (Sample Mass tab,
  X-ray tube / filter advisor). They are converted from `XAFSmass/data/Henke.npz`.
  The Sample Mass method (formula and weight-% parsing, μ/ρ from f″, edge step,
  foil thickness) follows XAFSmass.
- Source: https://github.com/kklmn/XAFSmass
- Original data: B. L. Henke, E. M. Gullikson and J. C. Davis, "X-ray interactions:
  photoabsorption, scattering, transmission, and reflection at E = 50-30000 eV,
  Z = 1-92", *At. Data Nucl. Data Tables* **54**, 181-342 (1993); CXRO, Lawrence
  Berkeley National Laboratory.
